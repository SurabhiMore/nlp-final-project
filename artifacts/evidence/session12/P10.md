What happened:

- Wrong answer: "What is your most famous painting?" got The Bedroom. The cited passage calls it
  "perhaps the most famous depiction of a bedroom in Western art history", and the model read that
  as his most famous painting. It is cited, so the reply check could not catch it.
- The Starry Night year (1889), where the T. rex lived and when it lived are backed by the
  passages cited.
- "Can you roar?" was heard as "Can you road?"; P10 fixed it in the transcript box and the roar
  played.
- The question about cars was declined as off topic, and the 2026 midterms as after its time,
  with nothing made up.
